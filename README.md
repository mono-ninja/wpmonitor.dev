# [WPMonitor.dev](https://wpmonitor.dev/)

WPMonitor is a tool for tracking statistics and analytics of WordPress plugins, themes, and authors.  
Its goal is to help developers monitor the performance of their products: installs, rankings, trends, and other key metrics.

## 📊 What WPMonitor Tracks
- **Active Installs** — number of active installations
- **Downloads** — total download count (including updates)
- **Rank** — position of the plugin/theme within its category or cluster
- **Trends** — changes over the last 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days
- **Author Rankings** — ranking of plugin/theme authors
- **Tags & Categories** — analytics of tags and categories
